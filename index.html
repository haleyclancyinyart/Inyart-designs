The Person Behind the Work

A little about me.

Hi! I'm the artist and designer behind Inyart Designs, an independent creative studio based in Belleville, Illinois.

With a background in studio art and an MFA in Drawing, I bring an artist's perspective to graphic design and social media marketing.

I believe the best creative work is intentional, personal, and made with a little curiosity. Whether it's a logo, race shirt, or an entire social media campaign, I enjoy turning ideas into something meaningful.
